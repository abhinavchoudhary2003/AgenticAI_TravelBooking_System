## 📍 Must‑See Attractions  

### Jammu  
1. **Raghunath Temple** – massive Hindu complex; best visited early morning.  
2. **Mubarak Mandi Palace** – historic palace‑museum, great for photography.  
3. **Bahu Fort & Bagh-e-Bahu** – lakeside garden, perfect for a sunset stroll.  
4. **Amar Mahal Museum & Library** – art & cultural exhibits.  

### En‑route Highlights  
| Stop | What to do |
|------|------------|
| **Patnitop** (≈ 2 h from Jammu) | Tea at **Milan Café**, short hike to **Patnitop Viewpoint**. |
| **Kangra** (≈ 3 h from Patnitop) | Explore **Kangra Fort**, **Masroor Rock‑cut Temple**. |
| **Barog Tunnel** (≈ 1 h from Kangra) | Famous 1 km railway tunnel – quick photo stop. |

### Shimla  
1. **The Ridge** – panoramic view of the Himalayas; walk to **Christ Church**.  
2. **Mall Road** – shopping, street food, colonial architecture.  
3. **Jakhu Temple** – giant Hanuman statue, trek or taxi up (2 km).  
4. **Viceregal Lodge (Indian Institute of Advanced Study)** – guided heritage tour (₹150).  
5. **Kufri** – adventure sports (skiing in winter, horse‑riding & trekking in summer).
