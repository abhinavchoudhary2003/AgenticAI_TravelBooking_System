## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Shimla**  
**How to get there**  
- Fly into **Chandigarh International Airport (IXC)** (most major Indian carriers) – 1 h 30 min from Delhi.  
- From Chandigarh, take a **private taxi / shared Volvo** to Shimla (≈ 4 h, 115 km) or a **Scenic Toy Train** from Kalka (if you love vintage rail).  

**Morning** – Check‑in, freshen up. Recommended hotels (mid‑range to luxury):  

| Hotel | Category | Why it’s great |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **The Oberoi Cecil** | Luxury | Colonial charm, central location, spa |
| **Hotel Willow Banks** | Mid‑range | River view, rooftop restaurant |
| **Hotel Comet** | Budget | Clean, friendly staff, close to Mall Road |

**Afternoon** – Stroll the **Mall Road**, explore the **Ridge**, and visit the **Christ Church**.  

**Evening** – Ride the **Jakhu Ropeway** (or hike up to Jakhu Temple) for panoramic sunset views of the Himalayas.  

**Dinner** – Try local Himachali dishes at **Cafe Sol** (tandoori trout, sidu).  

---

### **Day 2 – Shimla → Mashobra → Naldehra (Apple‑Orchard Day)**  
**Morning** – Drive 30 km to **Mashobra** (≈ 1 h).  

- **Apple Orchard Walk** – Guided walk through blooming orchards (seasonal, March‑May).  
- **Hidimba Devi Temple** – Small, peaceful shrine with great photo ops.  

**Lunch** – Picnic in the orchard (pre‑packed by your hotel) or eat at **Mongolian Café** in Mashobra.  

**Afternoon** – Continue to **Naldehra** (15 km, 30 min).  

- **Golf Course** – One of the oldest in India; you can rent clubs for a quick 9‑hole round or simply enjoy the lush green meadows.  
- **Short Trek** – 2‑hour trek to **Hatu Peak** (if you’re up for it) for sweeping valley views.  

**Evening** – Return to Shimla (≈ 1 h).  

**Dinner** – Sample Himachali **dadach (potato dumplings)** at **Ashiana & Co**.  

---

### **Day 3 – Shimla → Kullu → Manali (Scenic Drive)**  
**Early start** – Depart Shimla at 07:00 am.  

- **Route:** NH 5 → Mandi → Kullu → Manali (≈ 250 km, 6‑7 h).  
- **Highlights on the way:**  
  - **Mandi** – “Varanasi of the Hills”; quick stop at **Rewalsar Lake** (Buddhist pilgrimage).  
  - **Kullu** – Famous for **Kullu Shawls**; stretch legs at **Raghunath Temple**.  

**Lunch** – Riverside dhaba in Kullu (try **Kullu trout**).  

**Afternoon** – Arrive in **Manali**; check‑in. Recommended stays:  

| Hotel | Category |
|-------|----------|
| **The Himalayan** (Manali) | Luxury – boutique, river view |
| **Hotel Snow Valley Resorts** | Mid‑range – central, rooftop pool |
| **Zostel Manali** | Budget – hostel vibe, great for backpackers |

**Evening** – Walk along **Mall Road**, shop for woollen caps & hand‑knitted socks.  

**Dinner** – **Johnson’s Café** (famous for pancakes & apple pie).  

---

### **Day 4 – Manali – Adventure & Culture**  
**Option A – Summer (May‑Oct)**  

- **Morning:** Head to **Solang Valley** (≈ 14 km). Activities: paragliding, zorbing, rope‑way, and **snow‑capped mountain views** (even in summer!).  
- **Late Morning:** **Rohtang Pass** (if open; requires permit) or the newer **Atal Tunnel** (open year‑round, 8 km). The tunnel shortens the drive to **Lahaul‑Spiti** and offers spectacular views.  

**Option B – Winter (Dec‑Feb)**  

- **Morning:** **Skiing / Snowboarding** at **Solang** (beginner slopes).  

**Afternoon** – Return to Manali for a cultural visit:  

- **Hadimba Devi Temple** (wooden architecture).  
- **Vashisht Hot Springs** – soak in natural geothermal pools (great for sore muscles).  

**Evening** – Attend a **Kinnauri folk dance** show at **Manali Cultural Centre** (if available).  

**Dinner** – Try the local **Kangra Rajma** at **Rasoi**.  

---

### **Day 5 – Manali → Sangla (Kinnaur Valley)**  
**Early departure** – 07:00 am.  

- **Route:** Manali → Kullu → Aut (NH 3) → **Sangla** (≈ 200 km, 5‑6 h).  
- **Scenic stops:**  
  - **Karcham** – beautiful **Sangla River** confluence.  
  - **Baba Nahar Singh Temple** – quick photo stop.  

**Afternoon** – Check‑in at a riverside resort. Recommended:  

| Resort | Category |
|--------|----------|
| **Sangla Heights** | Mid‑range – river view, homestay feel |
| **Apple Country Resort** | Luxury – spa, garden, organic meals |
| **The Sangla Inn** | Budget – clean, central |

**Evening** – Walk along the **Baspa River**, explore the **Sangla Bazaar** (handicrafts, apricot jam).  

**Dinner** – Fresh **apple & apricot chutney** with locally sourced trout.  

---

### **Day 6 – Sangla → Kalpa (Kinnaur’s Crown Jewel)**  

**Morning** – Short drive (≈ 70 km, 2 h) to **Kalpa**.  

- **Check‑in** at:  
  - **The Himalayan Village** (luxury eco‑resort)  
  - **Kalpa Heritage Resort** (mid‑range)  
  - **Hotel Snow View** (budget)  

**Mid‑Morning** – **Sunrise at Kalpa** – the view of **Kinnaur Kailash** is spectacular; best seen from **Suha Village** (short trek).  

**Late Morning** – Visit **Baba Harish Chandra Temple** and **Kalpa Monastery** (Buddhist).  

**Afternoon** – **Apple Orchard Walk** (Kinnaur is famous for its pink‑ish apples) and **Kinnaur Museum** (ethnology).  

**Evening** – Optional **river rafting** on the **Satluj** (if water levels permit) or a **cooking class** to learn Himachali dishes.  

**Dinner** – Feast on **Chana Madra**, **Babru**, and **local honey** desserts.  

---

### **Day 7 – Return to Delhi/Chandigarh**  

**Option 1 – Fly out**  

- **Kullu‑Manali Airport (KUU)** offers daily flights to **Delhi** (≈ 1 h 30 min) and **Chandigarh** (≈ 1 h). Book a morning flight (e.g., 09:00 am).  

**Option 2 – Road & Fly**  

- Drive back to **Chandigarh** (≈ 10‑12 h) – break the journey at **Mandi** for lunch.  
- Catch an evening flight from Chandigarh to your onward destination.  

**If you have extra time** – consider a quick side‑trip to **Chail** (historic palace, cricket ground) on the way back to Shimla.

## PRACTICAL TIPS & CHECKLIST  

| Category | Tips |
|----------|------|
| **Visas / Documents** | Indian citizens need only a photo ID. International travelers need a valid Indian visa. |
| **Weather** | Pack layers: light jacket for daytime, warm sweater for evenings. In winter, bring waterproof boots and gloves. |
| **Health** | Altitude sickness is rare below 3,500 m, but stay hydrated. Carry basic meds (pain reliever, motion‑sickness tablets). |
| **Transportation** | Hire a **private SUV/tempo** with a driver (≈ ₹4,500‑₹6,000 per day). For budget travelers, shared **Himachal Roadways** buses are reliable. |
| **Permits** | Rohtang/Atal Tunnel passes require an online permit (₹ 500‑₹ 1,000). Apply 2‑3 days in advance. |
| **Connectivity** | 4G works in most towns; remote villages may have spotty signal. Buy a local SIM (Airtel/Jio) at the airport. |
| **Cash** | ATMs are available in Shimla, Manali & Kullu. Carry some cash (₹2,000‑₹5,000) for small shops and rural areas. |
| **Safety** | Roads are mountainous; drive cautiously, especially after rain. Keep a basic first‑aid kit. |
| **Cultural etiquette** | Remove shoes before entering temples. Dress modestly (shoulders covered) in religious sites. |
| **Food** | Try local specialties: **Siddu**, **Dham**, **Trout**, **Apple chutney**, **Kinnauri butter tea**. |
