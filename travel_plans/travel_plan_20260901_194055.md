## PRACTICAL TIPS FOR A SMOOTH TRIP  

| Tip | Why it matters |
|-----|----------------|
| **Weather** | Shimla’s temperature swings 5‑20 °C. Pack layers, a wind‑proof jacket, and comfortable walking shoes. |
| **Altitude** | 2,200 m – stay hydrated, avoid heavy alcohol on Day 1. |
| **Cash** | Most small shops accept only cash; keep ₹2,000‑₹3,000 handy. ATMs are available on Mall Road. |
| **Connectivity** | 4G works well; consider a local SIM (Airtel/Vi) for navigation. |
| **Health** | Carry a basic kit (pain reliever, motion‑sickness tablets – the road can be winding). |
| **Local Etiquette** | Remove shoes when entering temples; ask before photographing locals. |
| **Travel Insurance** | Recommended for any adventure activity (e.g., Kufri rope‑way). |
| **Time Management** | Roads can be slow due to fog or landslides; always add a 30‑minute buffer to your schedule. |
